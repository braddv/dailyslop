How to read it

One model through time

The timeframe buttons change only the visible history window. They do not change the scoring model. Growth blends equity trend, volatility, small caps, emerging markets, credit and copper versus gold. Inflation pressure blends energy, copper, agriculture, Treasury yields, the dollar and gold. Scores inside ±15 remain neutral so the system does not force a false regime.

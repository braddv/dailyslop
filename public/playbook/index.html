How to use this page

Start with agreement, investigate disagreement

Confirmed means that layer agrees with the candidate’s direction. It does not mean the trade will work.

Mixed means the evidence is usable but not decisive. These names need a cleaner trigger or smaller risk.

Against means a major layer conflicts with the idea. Treat it as a watch, not a finished setup.
